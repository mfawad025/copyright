Services on file.

Every engagement is scoped as a case, not a subscription line item. Pick the service that matches what's happening to your content.

01

One-Off Takedown Filing

You send the infringing link, we build the notice and file it with the host, CDN or registrar.

  • Ownership verification
  • Formal §512 notice drafted & filed
  • Status tracked to resolution
02

Marketplace & Storefront Enforcement

For counterfeit listings and cloned stores copying product photography, copy or branding.

  • Filed via the marketplace's own IP process
  • Repeat-seller escalation
  • Search-result de-indexing request
03

Video & Audio Piracy Removal

Re-uploads, stream rips and mirrored copies of video or audio work.

  • Platform Content-ID / rights-manager filing
  • Mirror tracking across re-uploads
  • Coordinated multi-URL notice
04

Ongoing Monitoring

Recurring scans for new copies of your content so notices go out before it spreads.

  • Scheduled re-checks
  • New-infringement alerts
  • Auto-drafted follow-up notices
05

Counter-Notice Defense

If you've been hit with a bad-faith DMCA claim against your own original work, we help you respond.

  • Claim review
  • Formal counter-notice drafting
  • Platform correspondence handling
06

Bulk Case Handling

For agencies and brands managing infringement across many assets at once.

  • Batch submission workflow
  • Case dashboard & status log
  • Priority filing turnaround
