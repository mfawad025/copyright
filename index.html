Docket Summary

A case file, not a form letter.

Generic takedown templates get ignored. Every WardMark notice references the specific statute, the specific URL, and the specific proof of ownership — because that's what gets a host to act.

What goes in the file

  • Timestamped proof of original ownership
  • Side-by-side comparison of original vs. infringing copy
  • Full URL list, archived at time of filing
  • Correct legal contact per host / registrar

What you get back

  • A case number to track status
  • Copies of every notice filed on your behalf
  • Follow-up filings if a host misses the deadline
  • A plain-language update when content comes down
